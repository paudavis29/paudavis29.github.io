Section 01 — Subject Profile
Field Note — Roots

Born in Costa Rica, where the guiding philosophy is Pura Vida?Literally "pure life" — but really a way of moving through the world: stay present, don't sweat what you can't control, and appreciate what's actually in front of you.. It still shapes how I show up to the job.

Field Note — The Discovery

While investigating carrier fraud, pattern analysis on flagged carrier data surfaced a scheme that, once shut down, prevented nearly $5 million in losses. That case is the reason I'm in cybersecurity — it proved that finding what's hidden was the part of the job I actually cared about.

Field Note — The Detour

As a kid, the plan was to become an FBI agent. Life took a different route — but it eventually landed me in federal cyber defense work that scratched a lot of the same itch.

Field Note — Public Service, Interrupted

My role in federal cybersecurity ended sooner than planned, a result of broader government workforce changes — not performance. I left on good terms and completed a smooth transition of my responsibilities.

Field Note — Today

That thread carried me into enterprise security, where I now own MFA deployment, access lifecycle management, and CIS Controls auditing across a 1,000+ user hybrid environment. I hold an active TS/SCI clearance, CompTIA Security+, and the UNC Charlotte Cybersecurity Certificate (4.0 GPA), and I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

Off the record — When I'm not auditing access controls professionally, I'm doing it recreationally: on the pickleball court, and at home with two dogs who fail every audit but somehow still hold all the access.
