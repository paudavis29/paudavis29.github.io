I'm an Information Security Analyst with 4+ years across federal, financial, and enterprise environments, with a home base in identity and access management. My path here wasn't a straight line: I started in fraud prevention and carrier compliance investigation, where pattern analysis on flagged transactions and fraudulent carrier schemes kept exposing the same root cause — gaps in identity verification and access control. That's what pulled me into cybersecurity.

From there I moved through CISA, reviewing System Security Plans and Privacy Impact Assessments against NIST 800-series controls, before landing at IMC Companies, where I now own MFA deployment, access lifecycle management, and CIS Controls auditing across a 1,000+ user hybrid environment. I hold an active TS/SCI clearance, CompTIA Security+, and the UNC Charlotte Cybersecurity Certificate (4.0 GPA), and I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.
